## 2. Dataset Specification & Leakage Certification

* **Dataset:** Flickr8k (`flickr8k.token.txt`, `Flickr_8k.trainImages.txt`, `Flickr_8k.devImages.txt`, `Flickr_8k.testImages.txt`)
* **Split Partitioning:**
  * **Train:** $6{,}000$ images, $30{,}000$ paired captions
  * **Validation:** $1{,}000$ images, $5{,}000$ paired captions (used strictly for model selection and early stopping)
  * **Held-Out Test:** $1{,}000$ images, $5{,}000$ paired captions (evaluated strictly once after training completion)
* **Split Leakage Audit:** Disjoint image ID assertion:
  $$\text{Train} \cap \text{Val} = \emptyset, \quad \text{Train} \cap \text{Test} = \emptyset, \quad \text{Val} \cap \text{Test} = \emptyset$$
  $$\text{Captions per image} = 5 \text{ (exact uniform distribution)}$$
